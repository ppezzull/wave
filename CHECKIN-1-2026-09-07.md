**What are you thinking of building?**
wave: a social market for on-chain strategies. You write a strategy in plain English
("keep ETH/USDC balanced, halt if Chainlink deviates 1.5%"), an agent compiles it to
1inch SwapVM bytecode and ships it on-chain; other traders' swaps execute against it and
pay the maker. Likes are liquidity: the feed ranks by realized return. An agent watches
every strategy through a subgraph and can retune or halt them autonomously. Stack:
1inch SwapVM/Aqua (execution), The Graph (all data), Foundry (contracts), World
(human verification at the publish gate, the new work this event).
